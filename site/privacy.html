Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Sideline Standings (the website and the iPhone app) shows public youth-soccer standings, schedules and predictions. It's unofficial and isn't run by or affiliated with any league or club. Anyone can look up a team's standings; a free account saves your teams, alerts and picks on all your devices.

What we store

  • your email address (or Apple's private relay address if you chose "Hide My Email"),
  • the teams you starred, and any season predictions you make (only you can see them),
  • if you play Pick'em: your picks and the name you choose. Other players see your name and picks once a game has kicked off,
  • when your account was created and when you last used it,
  • if you turn on alerts: that device's notification address (from your browser, or from Apple for the iPhone app), which teams it gets alerts for, your quiet hours, and the last 60 alerts sent to it so the app can list them,
  • a record of what the app and site do (for example "alerts turned on" or "a pick was saved") so we can fix problems. It's kept for 60 days.

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. Sign-in is handled by Clerk, with Apple, Google or email. In the iPhone app, sign-in opens your iPhone's own sign-in screen, so your saved logins work; we never see your password. We don't sell or share your information, don't show ads, and don't track you across other apps or sites.

Deleting your account

Tap the gear (top right) for Settings, then Your account, then Delete account. Your account, saved teams, picks and alerts are removed. To stop alerts on one device, use Settings › Alerts › Turn off, or turn off notifications for Sideline in your phone's settings.

Kids

Sideline Standings is meant for parents and families. Accounts are for adults; we don't knowingly create accounts for children under 13.
