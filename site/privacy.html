Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Sideline Standings shows public youth-soccer standings, schedules and predictions. It's unofficial and isn't run by or affiliated with the ECNL or any club. You use it with a free account, so your teams stay on all your devices.

What we store

Your account keeps your starred teams on all your devices. We store:

  • your email address (or Apple's private relay address if you chose "Hide My Email"),
  • the teams you starred, and any Predict picks you make (only you can see them),
  • when your account was created and when you last used the site,
  • if you turn on alerts: that device's notification address, which teams it gets alerts for, and your quiet hours.

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. Sign-in is handled by Clerk, with Google or Apple if you choose them. We never see your password. We don't sell or share your information, don't show ads, and don't use it for anything except keeping your teams in sync.

Deleting your account

Tap the gear (top right) for Settings, then Your account, then Delete account. Your account, saved teams and alerts are removed. To stop alerts on one device, use Settings › Alerts › Turn off.

Kids

The site is meant for parents and families. Accounts are for adults; we don't knowingly create accounts for children under 13.
