Support

Need help with Sideline Standings, or found something wrong in a table or a score? Email support@sidelinestandings.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Where do the standings come from? The leagues' published schedules and results. Our tables are worked out from the posted scores and checked against the official tables every day.

How do I get alerts? Sign in, star your team, then tap Turn on alerts (or Settings › Alerts). In the iPhone app, allow notifications when asked. On a website on iPhone, add Sideline to your Home Screen first.

How do I stop alerts? Settings › Alerts › Turn off, or turn off notifications for Sideline in your phone's settings.

How do I delete my account? Settings › Your account › Delete account. Everything is removed.
